Who We Are

Bridging Kerala
and Finland

The Finland Malayalee Association (FIMA) was established in 2013 with the aim of bringing together the Malayalee community in Finland and creating a sense of belonging far from home. Since its beginnings, FIMA has grown into a platform for celebrating Kerala's rich cultural heritage, preserving traditions and building lasting connections among Malayalees across Finland.

Through cultural festivals, Onam celebrations, community gatherings, sporting events and various initiatives, the association brings people of different generations together while welcoming newcomers into the community.

Today, FIMA continues to serve as a bridge between our roots in Kerala and our lives in Finland, fostering friendship, unity and a shared sense of identity. Our mission is to keep our culture and traditions alive while building an inclusive, connected and vibrant community for present and future generations.

Digital Student Card & Discounts

Frank App for Students

If you are studying at a Finnish university or university of applied sciences (AMK), download the Frank App right after enrolling. It serves as your official digital student card across Finland and unlocks hundreds of student discounts on VR & Matkahuolto long-distance trains and buses, HSL/regional public transport, UniCafe and campus meals, retail stores, and everyday services.

Visit Frank.fi
How to Get Started
  • 1. Join your student union and pay the student union fee.
  • 2. Register on frank.fi with your Finnish university email.
  • 3. Download the Frank App (iOS / Android) to activate your digital student ID.
  • 4. Show the card for transport, food, and retail discounts nationwide.

Student Housing in Finland

Trusted platforms for newly arrived students looking for apartments, shared flats, or student housing across Finland.

Housing
Qasa
Safe and flexible rental marketplace in Finland offering verified furnished and unfurnished apartments, ideal for newcomers without a Finnish credit history.
Visit Qasa.fi
Student Housing
HOAS (Helsinki Region)
Foundation for Student Housing in the Helsinki Region — affordable shared flats and studio apartments in Helsinki, Espoo, and Vantaa. Apply as soon as you get your admission letter.
Visit Hoas.fi
Nationwide
SOA (All Finnish Cities)
Finnish Student Housing Association — find official regional student housing organizations across Finland (TOAS Tampere, TYS Turku, PSOAS Oulu, KOAS Jyväskylä, and more).
Visit Soa.fi

One of Finland's largest rental portals listing private apartments, studios, and shared homes across all major cities.
